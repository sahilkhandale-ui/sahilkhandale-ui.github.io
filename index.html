About Me

I’m a multidisciplinary designer, video editor, and creative developer with a strong foundation in Computer Engineering. My work connects functional UI/UX design, visual storytelling, and technical understanding—crafting digital experiences that look modern, feel natural, and solve real user problems.

In product design, I specialize in end-to-end design workflows: user research, wireframing, high-fidelity UI, design systems, and interactive prototypes. Whether designing scalable SaaS platforms or mobile apps, I prioritize usability, accessibility, and clean visual hierarchy.

Beyond interfaces, I produce high-impact video edits, motion graphics, and brand identities. From cinematic cuts and pacing to sound design in FL Studio and generative AI workflows, I combine creative experimentation with structured design to bring digital ideas to life.
